## Brand & Style

The brand personality is uncompromisingly authoritative, institutional, disciplined, and refined. Designed for premier Indian real-estate acquisitions, land holdings, luxury penthouses, and industrial corridors, this design system establishes absolute trust through clarity, structural precision, and the elimination of superfluous decorative noise.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with an **Architectural High-Contrast** framework:
- Replaces speculative marketing flash with archival-grade typography, disciplined data grids, and stark contrast.
- Visual confidence stems from rigid alignment, measured negative space, and absolute monochrome restraint.
- Conveys the gravity of high-value land registry, RERA-verified certifications, and institutional-grade valuation models.

## Colors

The palette operates under an absolute monochromatic mandate. Chromatic colors, tints, hues, gradients, and soft pastel fills are prohibited across all standard and operational UI surfaces.

- **Primary Canvas & Contrast**: The baseline background is pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed against an ink-black primary foundation (`#0A0A0A` / `#000000`).
- **Tonal Hierarchy**:
  - `Surface Canvas`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `Surface Subdued / Well`: `#FAFAFA`
  - `Surface Muted`: `#F4F4F5`
  - `Hairline Grid / Borders`: `#E5E5E5`
  - `Secondary Label / Metadata`: `#777777`
  - `Sub-headline / Supporting Body`: `#555555`
  - `Primary Body Text`: `#1A1A1A`
  - `Solid Fill / High-Emphasis Ink`: `#0A0A0A`
- **Functional Semantics**:
  - Verification, legal clearances, and RERA status indicators are never green, red, or blue. Instead, they rely on stark structural indicators: solid black badges with crisp white micro-copy, or sharp white pills bounded by crisp 1px borders in `#1A1A1A`.
  - Negative states or critical alerts are conveyed via heavy-weighted typography, underlined callouts, or high-contrast filled glyphs within `#0A0A0A` housings.

## Typography

The typographic engine uses **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric precision, clean terminal cuts, and clinical architectural legibility.

- **Indian Valuation Typography**: The Indian numbering system (Lakhs and Crores) and unit metrics (`₹`, `Cr`, `L`, `sq.ft`, `Guntha`, `Acre`) demand distinct hierarchy. Currency figures utilize tabular numeric features (`tnum`) with heavy-weight treatment (`price-hero`, `price-md`), paired with uppercase unit labels (`label-caps`) to eliminate ambiguity in transaction valuations.
- **Architectural Rhythm**: All titles, metadata keys, and section headers employ precise negative letter-spacing for headlines and deliberate wide tracking (`0.08em`) for uppercase structural markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored to a structural 12-column fixed/fluid hybrid architectural grid built on an 8-point vertical cadence. 

- **Grid Model**:
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column layout, max content boundary `1440px`, flanked by `margin: 3rem` (48px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout, fluid margins scaling between 24px and 32px, `gutter: 1rem` (16px).
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, bounded by `margin-sm: 1.25rem` (20px) and `gutter-sm: 1rem` (16px).
- **Rhythm & Information Density**:
  - Property listings and technical specification tables eschew arbitrary whitespace in favor of rigorous 1px boundary lines dividing compact, dense data nodes.
  - Section-to-section flow relies on generous, editorial negative space (`space-xl` / 40px+) to maintain gravitas and executive focus.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects skeuomorphic shadows, colorful ambient glows, and blurred glassmorphism. Structural depth is communicated purely through **hairline division, planar layering, and solid tonal shifts**.

- **Hairline Dividers (Tier 0)**: Elements, cards, and data matrices sit flush on the `#FFFFFF` canvas, separated only by 1px solid `#E5E5E5` hairline borders.
- **Planar Recesses (Tier 1)**: Subdued informational modules, comparison wells, and filtering sidebars use `#FAFAFA` or `#F4F4F5` flat surface fills with zero shadow.
- **Overlays & Floating Modals (Tier 2)**: Flyouts, filter dropdowns, and master legal drawers employ an ultra-crisp, surgical drop-shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`, framed completely by a 1px solid `#0A0A0A` or `#E5E5E5` boundary line.
- **Focus & Selection**: State elevation is conveyed through an inward or outward crisp 1px solid stroke expansion, never a diffuse glow.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly architectural, sharp, and disciplined. Soft, organic shapes and pill-shaped aesthetics are rejected in favor of calculated precision.

- **Level 1 (Soft / Architectural Minimal)**: Standard components (buttons, text inputs, card boundaries) use an ultra-minimal `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- **Sharp Surfaces (0px)**: Data tables, floor-plan viewers, property technical matrices, document drawers, and media tiles utilize `rounded-none` (`0px`) to emphasize planar construction and structural permanence.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#0A0A0A` background with crisp `#FFFFFF` text. Minimal `4px` corner radius. Height: `44px` (desktop) / `48px` (mobile touch targets). Zero shadow. On hover: background shifts to `#1A1A1A`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Pure `#FFFFFF` background with 1px solid `#0A0A0A` border. Text `#0A0A0A`. On hover: solid `#0A0A0A` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Subtle / Flat**: Flat `#FAFAFA` background, 1px solid `#E5E5E5`, text `#1A1A1A`. On hover: border transitions to `#0A0A0A`.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Verified Badges (RERA / Legal Title Checked)**: Solid `#0A0A0A` fill with `#FFFFFF` micro-text in `label-caps` (`11px`, tracking `0.08em`), with a minimal `2px` to `4px` corner radius.
- **Attribute Tags (e.g., "Freehold", "Agricultural", "Plot", "Penthouse")**: Transparent background with a disciplined 1px solid `#E5E5E5` border and `#1A1A1A` text in `body-sm`. Hover state deepens the border to `#0A0A0A`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Structure**: Rectangular input boxes (`44px` height), `4px` corner radius, pure white surface, and 1px hairline border in `#E5E5E5`.
- **State Changes**: On focus, the border shifts directly to an uncompromising 1px solid `#0A0A0A` with no ambient blur or ring offset.
- **Labels**: Floating or top-stacked labels rendered in `label-caps` using `#777777`.

### Cards & Property Listings
- **Frame**: Bounded by a crisp 1px solid `#E5E5E5` border on a `#FFFFFF` base. 
- **Media Presentation**: Real-estate photography and site elevations must be displayed in uncompromising rectangular frames (`rounded-none` or `4px`), never softened by rounded pill cutouts.
- **Data Matrix**: Floor areas, carpet dimensions, and pricing are separated into structured 2-column or 3-column cells bounded by internal 1px horizontal and vertical hairpins (`#E5E5E5`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Geometry**: Checkboxes feature a strict `2px` corner radius; radio buttons are circular.
- **Styling**: Inactive controls have a clean 1px solid border in `#777777` with a white core. Active checked states snap instantly to a solid `#0A0A0A` fill with crisp white vector glyphs.

### Real-Estate Specific Components
- **Rupee Ledger Rows**: High-density financial breakdowns (Carpet Area, Stamp Duty, GST, Maintenance) arranged in an alternating border-bottom ledger with aligned `tnum` values.
- **RERA Compliance Stamp**: A clean, technical rectangular node containing the RERA ID, certificate validity, and archival timestamp, separated by 1px `#E5E5E5` borders.
