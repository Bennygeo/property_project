Frequently Asked Questions About Media Wallet

Everything you need to know about purchasing points, accessing 4K HDR walkthroughs, and tax invoicing.

help_outline

How do media points work?

Standard property photos and basic floor plans are always completely free. 2 Media Points (₹100) unlock complete 4K HDR walkthrough tours, drone aerial surveys, and cadastral survey dossiers with permanent lifetime access.

schedule

Do my wallet points ever expire?

No. Points in your Mannidam wallet have lifetime validity and will never expire, allowing you to research properties at your own pace across Chennai and other South Indian hubs.

receipt

Can I claim GST input tax credit?

Yes! Enable the Corporate GST toggle at checkout and enter your 15-digit GSTIN number. We will issue a tax invoice with 18% SAC 998314 digital real estate advisory code for claiming ITC.

shield

What is the 100% Title Guarantee?

If any property whose dossier you unlock is proven to possess an encumbered or disputed cadastral title, our concierge will immediately refund 100% of the points spent to your wallet.
